## Target audience segments

- **The first-year freelancer:** Recently went independent, still figuring out quarterly taxes, and keeps income in one bank account and receipts in a phone.
- **The established solo consultant:** Several regular clients and steady income, but no clear view of which clients or services are worth the time.
- **The mileage-heavy contractor:** Drives to clients or job sites and needs mileage and expenses kept tidy for tax time.
- **The side-business earner:** Has a 1099 income stream next to a job and wants the side business to be organized from day one.

## Common objections and honest responses

**"I just use my bank app."**  
A bank app shows money in and out. This workbook adds client profitability, mileage, estimated-tax planning and a year-end package on top. If the bank app already answers those questions for you, you may not need it.

**"I am not a spreadsheet person."**  
The formulas and dashboards are already built; you log income, expenses and mileage. Each edition has a Field Guide and a Quick Start. It needs desktop Excel.

**"I already use accounting software."**  
Accounting software records transactions. This is an owner's analysis tool: client profitability, forecasting, tax planning. Some people use both; some only need one.

**"Which edition do I need?"**  
Essential (11 sheets) covers clients, revenue, expenses, mileage, client performance, tax set-aside and a dashboard. Elite (26 sheets) adds invoices, receivables, cash flow, forecasting, an estimated tax center, a quarterly tax planner and a year-end tax package.

**"Will it lower my taxes?"**  
No tool can promise that. It helps you track deductible expenses and plan estimated taxes; your tax preparer decides what applies to you.

**"Is it for US contractors only?"**  
It is built around US 1099 work and estimated taxes. Contractors elsewhere should check the Field Guide first.

## Questions to answer before recommending an edition

- Do you only need income, expenses, mileage and a tax set-aside, or also invoices, cash flow and forecasting?
- How many clients do you work with?
- Do you pay quarterly estimated taxes?
- Do you drive for work?
- Do you want a year-end package for your tax preparer?

## Recommended edition by situation (from verified contents)

- **Essential:** Contractors who want client and revenue tracking, expenses, a mileage log, client performance, a tax set-aside estimate and a dashboard.
- **Elite:** Contractors who also want invoices and receivables, cash flow, monthly profit, client and service profitability, forecasting, an estimated tax center, a quarterly planner and a year-end tax package.
- **Complete:** Contractors who want both editions: start simple and grow into the full system.

## When the product is probably not a good fit

- People without desktop Microsoft Excel
- Contractors who want invoicing, payments or bank syncing in one app
- Anyone expecting tax advice — the tax figures are planning estimates
- Businesses with employees and payroll, which need different tools

Saying so builds trust. A buyer who is not a fit is a refund, not a commission.
