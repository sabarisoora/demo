## Target audience segments

- **The owner-electrician:** Still on the tools most days, quotes jobs from experience and invoices at night.
- **The growing service shop:** Several electricians, a mix of service calls and projects, and no side-by-side view of who produces what.
- **The project-focused contractor:** Bids larger jobs and needs to know which ones actually pay.
- **The office manager:** Handles invoicing and receivables and wants a clear monthly picture.

## Common objections and honest responses

**"My field service app already has reports."**  
Field apps dispatch and invoice. This workbook is the owner's view of job profit, electrician performance, leads, receivables aging, cash flow and tax set-aside. If you already see that, you may not need it.

**"I am not an Excel person."**  
The dashboards and formulas are built; you log jobs and expenses. Each edition includes a Field Guide and a Quick Start. It needs desktop Excel.

**"I am a one-person shop."**  
Essential is built for the core numbers: jobs, expenses, a dashboard, a health snapshot and tax set-aside.

**"Which edition do I need?"**  
Essential (8 sheets) covers job revenue, expenses, tax set-aside, a dashboard and a health snapshot. Elite (24 sheets) adds leads, job management, electrician performance, customers, AR aging, cash flow, KPIs, forecasting and benchmarking.

**"Will it make my jobs more profitable?"**  
No tool can promise that. It shows job costs and margins so you can quote and manage with real numbers.

**"Does it dispatch or invoice?"**  
No. It is an analysis workbook, not field service software.

## Questions to answer before recommending an edition

- Do you work alone or run crews?
- Do you track leads today?
- Are overdue invoices a problem?
- Do you want to compare electricians?
- Do you have desktop Excel?

## Recommended edition by situation (from verified contents)

- **Essential:** Contractors who want job revenue, expenses, a dashboard, a business health snapshot and a tax set-aside estimate.
- **Elite:** Contractors who also want leads, job management, electrician performance, customers, AR aging, cash flow, a KPI scorecard, forecasting and benchmarking.
- **Complete:** Contractors who want both editions in one package.

## When the product is probably not a good fit

- Contractors without desktop Microsoft Excel
- Anyone needing dispatch, scheduling or invoicing software
- Large firms requiring multi-user, real-time reporting
- Owners who will not log jobs regularly

Saying so builds trust. A buyer who is not a fit is a refund, not a commission.
