## Target audience segments

- **The single-listing host:** Rents out one property or a spare unit and wants to know whether it really pays after cleaning, fees and repairs.
- **The growing multi-property host:** Runs several listings and needs to compare them side by side.
- **The co-host or small manager:** Manages listings for owners and wants clean monthly numbers to share.
- **The investor-host:** Bought a property for short-term rental and wants to track return on investment.

## Common objections and honest responses

**"Airbnb already shows my earnings."**  
The platform shows payouts. This workbook adds expenses, cleaning, maintenance, property profitability, ROI and tax set-aside. If payouts are all you need, you may not need it.

**"I am not an Excel person."**  
The dashboards and formulas are already built; you log bookings and expenses. Each edition includes a Field Guide and a Quick Start. It needs desktop Excel.

**"I use a property management tool."**  
Those tools run bookings and messaging. This is an owner's analysis workbook for profit, ROI and taxes. They can work side by side.

**"Which edition do I need?"**  
Essential (10 sheets) covers properties, booking revenue, expenses, property performance, tax set-aside and a dashboard. Elite (25 sheets) adds occupancy, ADR and RevPAR analytics, cleaning and maintenance, ROI, rankings and a profit leak detector.

**"Will it increase my bookings?"**  
No. It does not touch your listings. It shows your numbers so you can decide what to change.

**"Does it connect to Airbnb?"**  
No. It is an Excel workbook; you enter or paste your booking data.

## Questions to answer before recommending an edition

- How many properties do you run?
- Do you want ADR, RevPAR and occupancy analytics, or just profit per property?
- Do you track cleaning and maintenance separately?
- Do you want ROI per property?
- Do you have desktop Excel?

## Recommended edition by situation (from verified contents)

- **Essential:** Hosts who want properties, booking revenue, expenses, property performance, a tax set-aside estimate and a dashboard.
- **Elite:** Hosts who also want occupancy, cleaning operations, maintenance, ADR and RevPAR analytics, ROI, property rankings, forecasting and a profit leak detector.
- **Complete:** Hosts who want both editions in one package.

## When the product is probably not a good fit

- Hosts without desktop Microsoft Excel
- Anyone wanting automatic booking imports or channel management
- Large property managers needing multi-user cloud reporting
- Hosts looking for pricing or listing optimization software

Saying so builds trust. A buyer who is not a fit is a refund, not a commission.
