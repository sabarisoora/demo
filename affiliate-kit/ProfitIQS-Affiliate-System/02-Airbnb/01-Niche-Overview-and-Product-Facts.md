## Audience

Airbnb and short-term rental hosts, co-hosts and small property managers running one or more listings.

## Buyer profiles (HYPOTHESIS)

**The single-listing host.** Rents out one property or a spare unit and wants to know whether it really pays after cleaning, fees and repairs.

**The growing multi-property host.** Runs several listings and needs to compare them side by side.

**The co-host or small manager.** Manages listings for owners and wants clean monthly numbers to share.

**The investor-host.** Bought a property for short-term rental and wants to track return on investment.

## Business problems in this industry (HYPOTHESIS)

General industry context to shape your angle. Do not present these as facts about the buyer or results of the product.

- **Revenue that hides the real profit.** Payouts look healthy until cleaning, platform fees, supplies and repairs are subtracted.
- **Properties that do not pull their weight.** With several listings, one weak property can quietly drag down the whole portfolio.
- **Occupancy without context.** A full calendar at a low nightly rate can earn less than fewer, better-priced nights.
- **Cleaning and maintenance costs.** Turnovers and repairs are frequent, small and easy to lose track of.
- **No clear return on the property.** Hosts often know the payout but not the return on what they invested.
- **Tax season paperwork.** Booking income, expenses and deductible costs end up scattered across platforms and receipts.

## Buyer motivations (HYPOTHESIS)

## Why someone might consider a business intelligence system

The common thread is visibility: Airbnb hosts usually have the data (repair orders, invoices, receipts) but not one place that turns it into decisions. The Airbnb Business Intelligence System™ gives them a ready-built Excel workbook for that (VERIFIED: dashboards, reports and tax set-aside built in). Whether it helps depends on the owner entering data and reviewing it regularly.

## Key numbers this audience cares about (educational)

- **Occupancy rate:** The share of available nights that were booked.
- **ADR (average daily rate):** Average booking revenue per booked night.
- **RevPAR:** Revenue per available night — occupancy and nightly rate combined into one number.
- **Profit per property:** What each listing earns after cleaning, fees, supplies and repairs.
- **Return on investment:** Profit compared with what you put into the property.
- **Cleaning cost per turnover:** What each changeover costs, which adds up fast with short stays.
