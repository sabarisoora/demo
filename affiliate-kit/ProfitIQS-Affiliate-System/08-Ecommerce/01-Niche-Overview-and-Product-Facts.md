## Audience

Online store owners selling on Shopify, Amazon, Etsy, TikTok Shop and other channels.

## Buyer profiles (HYPOTHESIS)

**The solo Shopify seller.** Runs the store alone, buys ads, ships orders and checks payouts, but not profit per product.

**The multi-channel seller.** Sells on several platforms and wants to know which channel actually makes money.

**The inventory-heavy brand.** Holds stock and needs purchase orders, suppliers and dead stock in view.

**The side-hustle store.** Small but growing store that wants good habits from the start.

## Business problems in this industry (HYPOTHESIS)

General industry context to shape your angle. Do not present these as facts about the buyer or results of the product.

- **Revenue that hides the real profit.** Sales look strong until product cost, shipping, fees, ads and refunds are counted.
- **Ads that may not pay back.** Ad spend is easy to increase and hard to judge without return per channel.
- **Products that lose money.** A best-seller by volume can be a weak product by margin.
- **Cash tied up in stock.** Slow-moving inventory holds cash that could be used elsewhere.
- **Refunds and returns.** Returns eat margin and are rarely analyzed by reason.
- **Tax-time paperwork.** Platform payouts, fees and receipts end up scattered by year end.

## Buyer motivations (HYPOTHESIS)

- Know real profit per product and channel
- Judge whether ads pay back
- Free up cash tied in stock
- Understand refunds and returns
- Be organized for taxes

## Why someone might consider a business intelligence system

The common thread is visibility: online store owners usually have the data (repair orders, invoices, receipts) but not one place that turns it into decisions. The Ecommerce Business Intelligence System™ gives them a ready-built Excel workbook for that (VERIFIED: dashboards, reports and tax set-aside built in). Whether it helps depends on the owner entering data and reviewing it regularly.

## Key numbers this audience cares about (educational)

- **Average order value (AOV):** Revenue divided by the number of orders.
- **ROAS:** Revenue generated for each dollar of ad spend.
- **SKU margin:** What each product keeps after its cost.
- **Refund rate:** Refunds as a share of orders or revenue.
- **Inventory value:** The cost value of stock on hand.
- **Customer lifetime value:** What a customer spends over all their orders.
