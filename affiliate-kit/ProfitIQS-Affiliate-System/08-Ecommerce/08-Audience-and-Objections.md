## Target audience segments

- **The solo Shopify seller:** Runs the store alone, buys ads, ships orders and checks payouts, but not profit per product.
- **The multi-channel seller:** Sells on several platforms and wants to know which channel actually makes money.
- **The inventory-heavy brand:** Holds stock and needs purchase orders, suppliers and dead stock in view.
- **The side-hustle store:** Small but growing store that wants good habits from the start.

## Common objections and honest responses

**"Shopify already shows my sales."**  
The platform shows sales. This workbook adds product cost, ads, refunds, inventory, cash flow and tax set-aside to show profit. If you already see that, you may not need it.

**"I am not an Excel person."**  
The dashboards and formulas are built; you log or paste sales and costs. Each edition includes a Field Guide and a Quick Start. It needs desktop Excel.

**"Does it connect to my store?"**  
No. It is an Excel workbook; you enter or paste exported data.

**"Which edition do I need?"**  
Essential (9 sheets) covers products, sales, costs, a dashboard, a health snapshot and tax set-aside. Elite (26 sheets) adds inventory, purchase orders, suppliers, sales channels, ad spend, refunds, cash flow, financial statements, SKU profitability and customer analytics.

**"Will it increase my sales?"**  
No. It shows your numbers so you can decide what to promote, reorder or cut.

**"I sell on several channels."**  
Elite includes a sales channels sheet to compare them; Essential tracks sales overall.

## Questions to answer before recommending an edition

- How many products and channels do you sell?
- Do you hold inventory?
- Do you run paid ads?
- Are refunds a noticeable cost?
- Do you have desktop Excel?

## Recommended edition by situation (from verified contents)

- **Essential:** Sellers who want product setup, sales and cost tracking, a dashboard, a business health snapshot and a tax set-aside estimate.
- **Elite:** Sellers who also want inventory, purchase orders, suppliers, sales channels, ad spend, refunds, cash flow, financial statements, SKU profitability and customer analytics.
- **Complete:** Sellers who want both editions in one package.

## When the product is probably not a good fit

- Sellers without desktop Microsoft Excel
- Stores wanting automatic order syncing or real-time dashboards
- Large brands needing inventory management software
- Sellers who will not export or enter their data

Saying so builds trust. A buyer who is not a fit is a refund, not a commission.
