## Target audience segments

- **The remodeling contractor:** Runs several residential projects at once and estimates from experience.
- **The small general contractor:** Manages subcontractors, change orders and progress billing across a handful of jobs.
- **The owner-operator builder:** Works on site during the day and does job costing at night, if at all.
- **The office manager:** Keeps the books and wants a clean view of project profit and work in progress.

## Common objections and honest responses

**"My estimating software already does this."**  
Estimating tools build the bid. This workbook tracks what actually happens: job costs, WIP, change orders, receivables, cash flow and tax set-aside. If your current tools already show that, you may not need it.

**"I am not an Excel person."**  
The dashboards and formulas are built; you log projects and costs. Each edition includes a Field Guide and a Quick Start. It needs desktop Excel.

**"Job costing takes too long."**  
Fair. The workbook needs costs entered against projects. Contractors who already collect receipts by job will find it straightforward.

**"Which edition do I need?"**  
Essential (8 sheets) covers project entry, expenses, tax set-aside, a dashboard and a health snapshot. Elite (25 sheets) adds the project portfolio, job cost center, WIP, change orders, project profitability, receivables, cash flow and forecasting.

**"Will it make my jobs more profitable?"**  
No tool can promise that. It shows job costs and margins clearly so you can bid and manage with real numbers.

**"Is it full construction accounting?"**  
No. It is an owner's analysis workbook, not accounting or project-management software.

## Questions to answer before recommending an edition

- How many projects do you run at once?
- Do you track change orders today?
- Do you need a WIP view?
- Is cash flow between draws a concern?
- Do you have desktop Excel?

## Recommended edition by situation (from verified contents)

- **Essential:** Contractors who want project entry, expenses, a dashboard, a business health snapshot and a tax set-aside estimate.
- **Elite:** Contractors who also want a project portfolio, job cost center, WIP, change orders, project profitability, receivables, cash flow, forecasting and benchmarking.
- **Complete:** Contractors who want both editions in one package.

## When the product is probably not a good fit

- Contractors without desktop Microsoft Excel
- Firms needing certified payroll, lien management or full construction accounting
- Large GCs requiring multi-user, real-time project systems
- Anyone who will not record costs against projects

Saying so builds trust. A buyer who is not a fit is a refund, not a commission.
