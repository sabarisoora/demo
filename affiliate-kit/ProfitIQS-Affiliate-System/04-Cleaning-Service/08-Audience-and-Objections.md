## Target audience segments

- **The owner-cleaner:** Still cleans most days, quotes jobs from experience and does the admin at night.
- **The crew-based company:** Runs several crews and needs to see crew productivity and job profit.
- **The commercial contract cleaner:** Works on recurring contracts and wants recurring revenue and contract terms in view.
- **The growing residential service:** Has a regular customer base and wants to keep customers coming back.

## Common objections and honest responses

**"My scheduling app already tracks jobs."**  
Scheduling apps run the calendar. This workbook adds job profitability, crew productivity, recurring revenue, cash flow and tax set-aside. If your app already answers those, you may not need it.

**"I am not an Excel person."**  
The formulas and dashboards are built; you log jobs and expenses. Each edition includes a Field Guide and a Quick Start. It needs desktop Excel.

**"I am too small for this."**  
Essential is designed for the core numbers: jobs, expenses, a dashboard, a health snapshot and tax set-aside. Elite is for owners managing crews and contracts.

**"Which edition do I need?"**  
Essential (8 sheets) covers job revenue, expenses, tax set-aside, a dashboard and a health snapshot. Elite (27 sheets) adds customers and retention, crews and productivity, job profitability, contracts, recurring revenue, receivables, cash flow and forecasting.

**"Will it win me more clients?"**  
No. It shows your numbers so you can decide what to price, keep or change.

**"Does it schedule my crews?"**  
No. It is an analysis workbook, not scheduling software.

## Questions to answer before recommending an edition

- Do you run crews or clean yourself?
- Do you have recurring contracts?
- Do you want job-by-job profitability?
- Do you need receivables and cash-flow tracking?
- Do you have desktop Excel?

## Recommended edition by situation (from verified contents)

- **Essential:** Owners who want job revenue, expenses, a dashboard, a business health snapshot and a tax set-aside estimate.
- **Elite:** Owners who also want customer retention, crew productivity, job profitability, contracts, recurring revenue, receivables, cash flow and forecasting.
- **Complete:** Owners who want both editions in one package.

## When the product is probably not a good fit

- Owners without desktop Microsoft Excel
- Anyone needing scheduling, dispatch or invoicing software
- Franchise systems requiring multi-location cloud reporting
- Owners who will not log jobs regularly

Saying so builds trust. A buyer who is not a fit is a refund, not a commission.
