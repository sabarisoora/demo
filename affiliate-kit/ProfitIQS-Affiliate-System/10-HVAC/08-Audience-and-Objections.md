## Target audience segments

- **The owner-technician:** Runs service calls and installs, quotes replacements and does the books at night.
- **The growing service company:** Several technicians and trucks, a maintenance agreement base, and no side-by-side view of performance.
- **The install-focused contractor:** Lives on replacement and installation work and wants to see margins by job.
- **The office or service manager:** Tracks agreements, estimates and receivables for the owner.

## Common objections and honest responses

**"My dispatch software already has reports."**  
Dispatch tools run calls and invoices. This workbook is the owner's view across service calls, installations, agreements, technicians, inventory, receivables, cash flow and taxes. If you already see that, you may not need it.

**"I am not an Excel person."**  
The dashboards and formulas are built; you log calls, jobs and expenses. Each edition includes a Field Guide and a Quick Start. It needs desktop Excel.

**"Data entry takes time."**  
Fair. Owners who already export from their field software will find it quicker; the Quick Start shows what to enter first.

**"Which edition do I need?"**  
Essential (8 sheets) covers job revenue, expenses, tax set-aside, a dashboard and a health snapshot. Elite (30 sheets) adds leads, estimates, work orders, service calls, technicians, customers, maintenance agreements, installations, inventory, fleet and labor, cash flow, receivables and forecasting.

**"Will it get me through the slow season?"**  
No tool can promise that. It shows monthly numbers so you can plan for it.

**"Does it dispatch technicians?"**  
No. It is an analysis workbook, not dispatch software.

## Questions to answer before recommending an edition

- Do you run service calls, installs or both?
- Do you sell maintenance agreements?
- How many technicians do you have?
- Do you follow up estimates today?
- Do you have desktop Excel?

## Recommended edition by situation (from verified contents)

- **Essential:** Contractors who want job revenue, expenses, a dashboard, a business health snapshot and a tax set-aside estimate.
- **Elite:** Contractors who also want leads, estimates, work orders, service calls, technician performance, customers, maintenance agreements, installations, inventory, fleet and labor, receivables, cash flow and forecasting.
- **Complete:** Contractors who want both editions in one package.

## When the product is probably not a good fit

- Contractors without desktop Microsoft Excel
- Anyone needing dispatch, scheduling or invoicing software
- Large multi-branch companies needing real-time reporting
- Owners who will not log calls and jobs regularly

Saying so builds trust. A buyer who is not a fit is a refund, not a commission.
