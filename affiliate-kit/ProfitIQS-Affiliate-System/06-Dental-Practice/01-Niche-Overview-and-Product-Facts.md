## Audience

Dental practice owners, associate-to-owner dentists and practice managers.

## Buyer profiles (HYPOTHESIS)

**The new practice owner.** Recently bought or opened a practice and wants a clear view of production, collections and costs.

**The established solo practice.** Busy schedule, steady patients, but no single view of how providers, hygiene and procedures perform.

**The practice manager.** Runs operations and reports to the dentist-owner every month.

**The growth-minded group.** Two or more providers and wants to compare them and plan growth.

## Business problems in this industry (HYPOTHESIS)

General industry context to shape your angle. Do not present these as facts about the buyer or results of the product.

- **Production is not collections.** Work produced and money collected can drift apart through insurance delays and patient balances.
- **New patients and recalls slipping.** A practice can be busy while recall patients quietly stop coming back.
- **Case acceptance blind spots.** Treatment presented but not accepted is revenue that never shows up.
- **Provider and hygiene performance.** Without a side-by-side view, it is hard to compare providers fairly.
- **Insurance claims and collections.** Outstanding claims and patient balances tie up cash.
- **Tax and accounting paperwork.** Payroll, supplies, equipment and marketing costs need to be organized for the accountant.

## Buyer motivations (HYPOTHESIS)

- See production, collections and patient flow together
- Keep recalls and new patients on track
- Compare providers and procedures fairly
- Get a handle on claims and collections
- Run a clear monthly review

## Why someone might consider a business intelligence system

The common thread is visibility: dental practice owners usually have the data (repair orders, invoices, receipts) but not one place that turns it into decisions. The Dental Practice Business Intelligence System™ gives them a ready-built Excel workbook for that (VERIFIED: dashboards, reports and tax set-aside built in). Whether it helps depends on the owner entering data and reviewing it regularly.

## Key numbers this audience cares about (educational)

- **Production:** The value of dentistry performed in a period.
- **Collections:** Money actually received, from patients and insurers.
- **Collection rate:** Collections as a share of production.
- **Case acceptance:** The share of presented treatment that patients accept.
- **Recall rate:** How many patients return for their recall visits.
- **New patients:** How many new patients the practice adds each month.
