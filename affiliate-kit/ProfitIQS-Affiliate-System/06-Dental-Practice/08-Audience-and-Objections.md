# Dental Practice — Audience and Objection Guide

## Target audience segments

- **The new practice owner:** Recently bought or opened a practice and wants a clear view of production, collections and costs.
- **The established solo practice:** Busy schedule, steady patients, but no single view of how providers, hygiene and procedures perform.
- **The practice manager:** Runs operations and reports to the dentist-owner every month.
- **The growth-minded group:** Two or more providers and wants to compare them and plan growth.

## Common objections and honest responses

**"My practice management software has reports."**  
Practice software runs scheduling, charting and billing. This workbook is an owner's business view across production, collections, providers, hygiene, payroll, supplies, marketing ROI and taxes. If your reports already cover that, you may not need it.

**"I am not an Excel person."**  
The dashboards and formulas are built; data is entered or pasted in. Each edition includes a Field Guide and a Quick Start. It needs desktop Excel.

**"Is patient data safe?"**  
It is a local Excel file on your computer. Keep patient-identifying information out of it unless your compliance setup allows it. [NEEDS CONFIRMATION: practice's own HIPAA policy]

**"Which edition do I need?"**  
Essential (8 sheets) covers revenue, expenses, tax set-aside, a dashboard and a health snapshot. Elite (42 sheets) adds patients, recalls, treatment and case acceptance, provider and hygiene productivity, procedure profitability, claims, collections, payroll, supplies, marketing ROI and forecasting.

**"Will it grow my practice?"**  
No tool can promise that. It shows the numbers so you can decide where to focus.

**"Does it connect to my practice software?"**  
No. It is an Excel workbook; data is entered or pasted in.

**"Is it a subscription?"**  
It is a downloadable Excel product. [NEEDS CONFIRMATION: final purchase terms on the Digistore24 sales page]

## Questions to answer before recommending an edition

- Do you want the core financial picture or the full practice view?
- How many providers and hygienists do you have?
- Do you track recalls and case acceptance today?
- Are claims and collections a concern?
- Do you have desktop Excel?

## Recommended edition by situation (from verified contents)

- **Essential:** Practices that want revenue, expenses, a dashboard, a business health snapshot and a tax set-aside estimate.
- **Elite:** Practices that also want patient, recall, treatment, case acceptance, provider, hygiene, procedure, claims, collections, payroll, supply, equipment and marketing ROI tracking.
- **Complete:** Practices that want both editions in one package.

## When the product is probably not a good fit

- Practices without desktop Microsoft Excel
- Anyone wanting automatic syncing with practice management software
- Practices that cannot keep patient data out of spreadsheets under their compliance policy
- Large DSOs needing enterprise reporting

Saying so builds trust. A buyer who is not a fit is a refund, not a commission.
