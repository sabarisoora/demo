# Affiliate Launch Checklist

## 1. Select a niche

- [ ] Choose the industry your audience belongs to
- [ ] Open that niche folder and read `README.md`

## 2. Review the product

- [ ] Read `01-Niche-Overview-and-Product-Facts.md` and `02-Tier-Comparison.md`
- [ ] Note which edition fits your audience

## 3. Access promotional assets

- [ ] Pick hooks, scripts, posts and emails
- [ ] Download the graphics from `graphics/`

## 4. Get the correct tracking link

- [ ] Sign in to Digistore24 and request promotion if required
- [ ] Copy the link for the correct niche and edition
- [ ] Replace every `[YOUR AFFILIATE LINK]`

## 5. Check the landing page

- [ ] The link opens the right product
- [ ] The page loads on mobile
- [ ] Prices match the kit

## 6. Test tracking (approved method)

- [ ] Click your own link once from a private browser window — do not buy through your own link to test
- [ ] Confirm the click appears in your Digistore24 reports (reporting can be delayed)

## 7. Publish

- [ ] Disclosure in every post, email and video
- [ ] Run the niche `09-Publishing-Checklist.md`

## 8. Monitor

- [ ] Check clicks, sales and refunds in Digistore24 weekly
- [ ] Note which hooks and channels get clicks; drop what does not work
- [ ] Watch for commission phase changes (17 January 2027, 17 April 2027)
