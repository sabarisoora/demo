# ProfitIQS Affiliate Program — Overview

## What ProfitIQS is

ProfitIQS makes downloadable Excel business intelligence systems for specific industries. Each product is a ready-built workbook with dashboards, reports and a tax set-aside estimate, plus a Field Guide and a Quick Start. Buyers enter their own data; the formulas are already in place.

## How the program works

1. You promote a ProfitIQS product with your own Digistore24 affiliate link.
2. A customer buys through your link on Digistore24.
3. Digistore24 records the sale and credits your commission on eligible orders.
4. You see clicks, sales, refunds and commissions in your Digistore24 account.

Launch commission: **90%** from 17 October 2026 to 16 January 2027, then 85% and 80% under the planned schedule. See `Commission-Guide.md`.

## Choosing a niche

Pick the industry your audience already belongs to. Promoting the HVAC system to HVAC contractors converts better, and is more honest, than promoting every niche to everyone. You can promote several niches, each with its own kit.
