## How actual payouts can differ

- Digistore24 may calculate commission on a net amount after its fees or VAT/sales tax.
- Refunds and chargebacks reverse commissions.
- Payout timing, thresholds and methods follow Digistore24's terms and your account settings.

## Recruiting copy rules

- You may say: "90% launch commission through 16 January 2027, then 85%, then 80% standard."
- Do not say: "earn $224.10 per sale guaranteed", "lifetime 90%", or any income promise.
