## Target audience segments

- **The busy owner-operator:** Runs one shop, still turns wrenches or writes service some days, and does the books at night. Knows the shop is busy but not which work actually pays.
- **The growing multi-bay shop:** Six or more bays, several technicians and a service advisor. Needs to see technician output, bay use and parts margin without buying a full shop-management suite.
- **The numbers-curious manager:** A service or general manager who reports to an owner and wants a clean monthly review instead of building spreadsheets from scratch.
- **The fleet-account shop:** Services commercial or fleet vehicles alongside retail customers and wants fleet work tracked separately.

## Common objections and honest responses

**"My shop management software already has reports."**  
Many shop systems focus on writing ROs and invoicing. This workbook is for the owner-level view across labor, parts, technicians, bays, expenses and taxes in one place. If your current reports already answer those questions, you may not need it.

**"I am not an Excel person."**  
The formulas, dashboards and reports are already built. You enter repair orders and expenses, and each edition includes a Field Guide and a Quick Start. It does require desktop Excel.

**"I do not have time to enter data twice."**  
That is a fair concern. The workbook needs repair orders and expenses entered (or pasted) in. Owners who already export data from their shop system will find it easier.

**"Which edition do I need?"**  
Essential covers repair orders, expenses, a dashboard, a health snapshot and tax set-aside. Elite is the full 41-sheet system, adding deeper analysis such as technicians, bays, parts, warranty, comebacks and fleet accounts. Complete includes both.

**"Will this make my shop more profitable?"**  
No tool can promise that. The workbook shows the numbers clearly; what changes is up to the decisions you make with them.

**"Does it replace my accountant?"**

## Questions to answer before recommending an edition

- Do you want the core numbers (repair orders, expenses, profit, tax set-aside) or a deeper analysis of technicians, bays and parts?
- How many technicians and bays do you run?
- Do you track warranty work, comebacks or fleet accounts separately today?
- Do you already have desktop Excel?
- Would you rather start simple and see if the habit sticks, or set up the full system now?

## Recommended edition by situation (from verified contents)

- **Essential:** Owners who want the core picture: repair orders, expenses, profit, a business health snapshot and a tax set-aside estimate.
- **Elite:** Shops that want to analyze technicians, service advisors, bays, labor and parts sales, inventory, warranty, comebacks and fleet accounts.
- **Complete:** Owners who want both editions: start with Essential and grow into Elite.

## When the product is probably not a good fit

- Shops that do not use, or cannot run, desktop Microsoft Excel
- Owners looking for software that writes repair orders, schedules or invoices customers
- Anyone expecting the tool to fix profit on its own without reviewing the numbers
- Multi-location groups that need live, multi-user cloud reporting

Saying so builds trust. A buyer who is not a fit is a refund, not a commission.
