## Target audience segments

- **The owner-operator:** Runs routes personally, sells contracts, and does the paperwork at night.
- **The multi-technician company:** Several technicians and routes, and no side-by-side view of route or technician profit.
- **The contract-heavy business:** Most revenue comes from recurring service contracts that need renewing.
- **The office manager:** Tracks contracts, receivables and chemical stock for the owner.

## Common objections and honest responses

**"My routing software already has reports."**  
Routing tools plan the day. This workbook is the owner's view of contracts, routes, technicians, chemical inventory, receivables, cash flow and taxes. If you already see that, you may not need it.

**"I am not an Excel person."**  
The dashboards and formulas are built; you log services and expenses. Each edition includes a Field Guide and a Quick Start. It needs desktop Excel.

**"I only have a few routes."**  
Essential covers the core: services, expenses, a dashboard, a health snapshot and tax set-aside.

**"Which edition do I need?"**  
Essential (8 sheets) covers service entry, expenses, tax set-aside, a dashboard and a health snapshot. Elite (25 sheets) adds customers, service contracts, a service call log, route management, technician performance, revenue analytics, chemical inventory, receivables, cash flow and forecasting.

**"Will it grow my customer base?"**  
No. It shows your numbers so you can decide which routes, contracts and services to focus on.

**"Does it plan my routes?"**  
No. It analyzes route profitability; it is not routing software.

## Questions to answer before recommending an edition

- How many routes and technicians do you run?
- How much of your revenue is recurring contracts?
- Do you track chemical inventory?
- Are unpaid invoices a concern?
- Do you have desktop Excel?

## Recommended edition by situation (from verified contents)

- **Essential:** Operators who want service entry, expenses, a dashboard, a business health snapshot and a tax set-aside estimate.
- **Elite:** Operators who also want customers, service contracts, a service call log, route management, technician performance, revenue analytics, chemical inventory, receivables, cash flow and forecasting.
- **Complete:** Operators who want both editions in one package.

## When the product is probably not a good fit

- Operators without desktop Microsoft Excel
- Anyone needing routing, scheduling or invoicing software
- Large multi-branch companies needing real-time reporting
- Owners who will not log services regularly

Saying so builds trust. A buyer who is not a fit is a refund, not a commission.
