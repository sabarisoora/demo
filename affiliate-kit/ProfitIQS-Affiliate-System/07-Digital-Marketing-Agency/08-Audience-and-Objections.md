## Target audience segments

- **The boutique agency owner:** Five to fifteen clients on retainers, still involved in delivery, and does finance at month end.
- **The freelancer turning agency:** Recently hired help and needs to see whether each client still pays after contractor costs.
- **The operations lead:** Tracks pipeline, retainers and receivables for the founder.
- **The specialist agency:** Sells a few core services and wants to know which one is most profitable.

## Common objections and honest responses

**"Our project tool already tracks this."**  
Project tools track tasks and time. This workbook is the owner's financial view: client and service profitability, retainers, pipeline, receivables, cash flow and tax set-aside. If you already have that, you may not need it.

**"I am not an Excel person."**  
The dashboards and formulas are built; you log revenue and costs. Each edition includes a Field Guide and a Quick Start. It needs desktop Excel.

**"We are too small."**  
Essential covers the core: client revenue, expenses, a dashboard, a health snapshot and tax set-aside. Elite is for agencies managing pipelines, retainers and several services.

**"Which edition do I need?"**  
Essential (8 sheets) covers client revenue, expenses, tax set-aside, a dashboard and a health snapshot. Elite (26 sheets) adds leads, sales pipeline, retainers, projects, service and client profitability, receivables, cash flow and forecasting.

**"Will it get us more clients?"**  
No. It shows your numbers so you can decide which clients and services to focus on.

**"Is it software we have to set up and maintain?"**  
No. It is a downloadable Excel workbook for the agency's numbers. It is not an AI or automation tool; you enter your data and the reports are built in.

## Questions to answer before recommending an edition

- How many clients and retainers do you run?
- Do you track leads and proposals?
- Do you sell several distinct services?
- Are overdue invoices a problem?
- Do you have desktop Excel?

## Recommended edition by situation (from verified contents)

- **Essential:** Agencies that want client revenue, expenses, a dashboard, a business health snapshot and a tax set-aside estimate.
- **Elite:** Agencies that also want leads, a sales pipeline, retainers, projects, service and client profitability, receivables, cash flow and forecasting.
- **Complete:** Agencies that want both editions in one package.

## When the product is probably not a good fit

- Agencies without desktop Microsoft Excel
- Teams wanting time tracking, task management or client reporting dashboards
- Large agencies needing multi-user, real-time financial systems
- Owners who will not log revenue and costs regularly

Saying so builds trust. A buyer who is not a fit is a refund, not a commission.
