# ProfitIQS Affiliate VSL — Script v1 (for review)

**Audience:** people who could promote ProfitIQS: creators, consultants, bookkeepers, agency owners, and anyone with an audience of small-business owners.
**Goal:** show them the product is real and worth promoting (problem → solution), explain the program and commission honestly, show everything in the kit, and send them to get their Affiliate Hub link.
**Length:** about 10½–11 minutes (≈1,415 spoken words plus visual beats). Exact timings come from the recorded voice.
**Look (new format, not the showroom or sheet tour):** *"The Affiliate Playbook"*. The light style again (white, pastel washes, magenta / purple / cyan, black text). A kit box sits on a bright desk. Each chapter pulls one folder out of the box, a progress rail across the top shows **Chapter 3 of 12**, and every number and rule lands on screen as it's spoken.
**Sources:** Master Affiliate Kit (program overview, commission guide, brand messaging, compliance, launch checklist, cold-email playbook) and the niche kits (product facts, tier comparisons, hooks, scripts). No invented results, earnings, testimonials or customer counts. Marketplace status is described as **pending**, never "approved".

## Chapter 8 · Your first week
**Visual:** a checklist that ticks itself, one box per spoken step; the wall of 21 niche tiles lights up on "pick the niche your audience lives in".

> Here's how to launch, step by step.
> Pick the niche your audience already lives in. HVAC content to HVAC contractors converts better, and it's more honest, than everything to everyone.
> There are twenty-one to choose from: ten ninety-nine contractors, Airbnb hosts, auto repair shops, cleaning services, construction, dental practices, digital marketing agencies, ecommerce, electrical contractors, HVAC, pest control, landscaping, lawn care, manufacturing, plumbing, property management, rental property, restaurants, roofing, small businesses, and trucking owner-operators.
> Read that niche's product facts and tier comparison.
> Pick your hooks, scripts, posts and emails, and download the graphics.
> Sign in to Digistore24, find the product, and copy your affiliate link for the right niche and edition.
> Check the page: it opens the right product, it loads on mobile, and the prices match.
> Test your link once, from a private browser window. Don't buy through your own link to test it.
> Then publish, with a clear disclosure in every post, email and video.
> And check your clicks, sales and refunds weekly. Keep what works. Drop what doesn't.

## Chapter 10 · The rules
**Visual:** rules appear as bright ✓ / ✗ cards; the ✗ cards get stamped.

> And the rules that keep all of this working.
> Disclose clearly, right next to your link, and say it in your videos.
> Describe the product honestly: an Excel system that needs desktop Excel.
> No fake reviews or testimonials. No income or results claims, for buyers or for affiliates.
> No spam, no impersonating ProfitIQS or Digistore24, and no bidding on the ProfitIQS brand name in paid search.
> No discounts, bonuses or guarantees that ProfitIQS hasn't approved.
> And no claims of integrations, automation or AI. The products don't have them, and that's fine.
> If you're ever unsure, leave it out, or ask.
