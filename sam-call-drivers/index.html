Questions

Before you book

What is an acting driver or call driver?

A driver who comes to drive your own vehicle. You keep your car; we send a trained driver for the hours, days or trip you need.

How fast can I get a driver?

For most city bookings we can arrange a driver the same day. For outstation trips, monthly drivers and events, booking a day earlier helps us send the best match.

Do you have drivers for buses and lorries?

Yes. We have drivers with heavy vehicle licences for buses, lorries, tippers and mini trucks, for T-board and own board vehicles.

Can I get a driver for a whole month?

Yes. Our monthly drivers work on contract for families and companies. If your driver takes leave, we send a replacement.

Do you also provide the car?

Yes. Our cab and rental service gives you a sedan, SUV, Innova or tempo traveller with a driver, for local and outstation trips.

How are charges decided?

Charges depend on the service, vehicle, hours and distance. Call or WhatsApp us and we tell you the rate before the trip starts.
