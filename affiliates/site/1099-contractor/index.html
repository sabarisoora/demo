Long description
Running a 1099 contracting business means answering questions like "Which clients are actually worth my time?" and "How much should I set aside for taxes?" — usually without clear numbers.

1099 Contractor Business Intelligence System™ is an industry-specific Excel workbook built for independent contractors, freelancers and self-employed professionals paid on 1099s. You enter your own data; the formulas, dashboards and reports are already built in.

Essential 2026 (11 sheets) covers the core:
• Client setup and revenue log
• Expense entry and mileage log
• Client performance view
• Tax set-aside estimate and deduction summary
• Dashboard, health snapshot and accountant export

Elite 2026 (26 sheets) adds deeper analysis:
• Client and service profitability
• Invoice tracker and accounts receivable
• Cash flow and monthly profit centers
• Estimated tax center and quarterly tax planner
• Forecasting, KPI scorecard and profit leak detector
• Year-end tax package and deduction maximizer

Complete 2026 includes both editions. Every edition comes with a Field Guide and a Quick Start guide, and sample data so you can see how everything works before entering your own. Estimated-tax and quarterly planning sheets show what to set aside as you go.

It is a planning and tracking tool, not tax, legal or financial advice.

Q: What is 1099 Contractor Business Intelligence System™?
