Long description
Running a HVAC business means answering questions like "Which service types and technicians are most profitable?" and "How many maintenance agreements are active or up for renewal?" — usually without clear numbers.

HVAC Business Intelligence System™ is an industry-specific Excel workbook built for hvac contractors and heating and cooling service business owners. You enter your own data; the formulas, dashboards and reports are already built in.

Essential 2026 (8 sheets) covers the core:
• Job revenue entry
• Expense entry
• Tax set-aside estimate and deduction summary
• Dashboard and business health snapshot
• Accountant export

Elite 2026 (30 sheets) adds deeper analysis:
• Lead tracker, estimate center and work order center
• Service call tracker and service analytics
• Technician performance and vehicle & labor center
• Maintenance agreements, installations and inventory
• Accounts receivable, cash flow, KPI scorecard and forecasting
• Tax reserve, deduction maximizer and accountant summary

Complete 2026 includes both editions. Every edition comes with a Field Guide and a Quick Start guide, and sample data so you can see how everything works before entering your own. Pick your country once and the tax set-aside rates, currency and VAT/GST labels follow from a built-in rate table.

It is a planning and tracking tool, not tax, legal or financial advice.
