How it works

From sign-up to your first promotion

  1. 01

    Explore the products

    Browse the industries below and open a product's resource page to see exactly what each edition contains.

  2. 02

    Check promotion availability

    Each product shows its affiliate status. Only promote products marked available, or confirmed for you in Digistore24.

  3. 03

    Join through Digistore24

    Create or sign in to your Digistore24 account. Some products may need vendor approval before you can promote them.

  4. 04

    Get your tracking link

    Your personal promotion link is generated inside your Digistore24 account. ProfitIQS cannot create it for you.

  5. 05

    Promote accurately

    Use the ready-made copy, images and banners, add your link, and include a clear affiliate disclosure.

  6. 06

    Track your results

    Clicks, sales, refunds and commissions are reported in your Digistore24 dashboard.

Not every product is automatically open for promotion. Availability and approval are managed in Digistore24.

Promotion guidelines

Promote with integrity

ProfitIQS products help owners see their numbers. Keep your promotion just as clear.

Do

  • Describe the products as they are: downloadable Excel workbooks with guides
  • Disclose your affiliate relationship clearly, close to the link
  • Use the editions' real contents when you compare them
  • Send people to the official sales page through your tracking link
  • Follow the marketing and privacy laws that apply to you and your audience

Don't

  • Promise income, savings, tax outcomes or business results
  • Write fake reviews, testimonials, screenshots or customer stories
  • Invent discounts, countdowns, scarcity or pricing
  • Present ProfitIQS products as tax, legal or accounting advice
  • Spam, bid on or imitate the ProfitIQS brand, or pose as ProfitIQS
  • Collect personal data without consent

Draft guidelines. These guidelines explain ProfitIQS's expectations. They do not replace Digistore24's affiliate terms or the laws that apply to you, which remain binding.

FAQ

Affiliate questions, answered

Anything account-specific — payouts, cookie windows, refunds — is set by Digistore24. Check your account or their terms.

What is ProfitIQS?

ProfitIQS makes downloadable Excel business intelligence systems for specific industries — 1099 Contractor, Airbnb, Auto Repair, Cleaning Service, Construction, Dental, Digital Marketing Agency, Ecommerce, Electrical Contractor, HVAC, Pest Control. Each workbook comes with a Field Guide and a Quick Start.

What products can I promote?

Every industry listed on this page has Essential, Elite and Complete 2026 editions. Promote a product once its status shows it is available, or once it is confirmed for you in Digistore24.

How does affiliate commission work?

When a customer buys through your personal Digistore24 link, Digistore24 records the sale and credits your commission on eligible orders.

What is the current commission rate?

The standard rate is 80% on eligible sales. Any temporary promotion is announced on this page with its dates; your Digistore24 account shows the rate that applies to you.

How do I get my affiliate link?

Sign in to Digistore24, find the ProfitIQS product you want to promote, and request or copy your promotion link there. Links are personal to your account.

How are sales tracked?

Through Digistore24. Tracking details such as cookie duration are set by the platform and the product settings — check them in your account.

Where do I see my earnings?

In your Digistore24 account, which reports clicks, sales, refunds and commissions.

Can I promote multiple industries?

Yes. Each industry has its own resource page and materials, so you can promote the ones that fit your audience.

Can I use the supplied marketing resources?

Yes — the copy, product images and banners on this portal are provided for promoting ProfitIQS products. Keep them accurate and add your own affiliate disclosure.

What marketing practices are prohibited?

Spam, fake reviews or testimonials, income or results promises, misleading pricing or discounts, unsupported tax or legal claims, and anything that impersonates ProfitIQS. See the promotion guidelines above.

Who handles customer support?

ProfitIQS handles product support for customers at support@profitiqs.com. Orders, payments and refunds are processed by Digistore24.

Where can I get affiliate assistance?

Email support@profitiqs.com with "Affiliate" in the subject line. For account, tracking or payout questions, contact Digistore24 support.

Questions about promoting ProfitIQS?

Email the team with "Affiliate" in the subject line.
